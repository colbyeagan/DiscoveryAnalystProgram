An example value. Enter the current target to match today. Other rates in the model are built from it.

Markets move on the surprise. Bond, stock, dollar, commodity and derivatives effects shrink as more of the move was expected. Money market and household loan rates follow the actual change.

IncreaseDecreaseBars show direction and relative size, not good or bad. Full bar is roughly a 150 bp surprise.

Sensitivities are simplified: bond prices come from pricing a par bond at the new yield, stock moves from a discount-rate valuation (price proportional to 1 / (discount rate minus growth)), and the rest from fixed pass-through ratios. Starting yields, spreads and loan rates are illustrative.

Not modeled: changes in earnings, growth expectations, central bank guidance and global rate moves. Household effects are shown for new loans; fixed-rate loans already in place do not reprice, and spending responds over roughly 12 to 18 months.
